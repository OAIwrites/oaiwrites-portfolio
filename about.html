The Background

My creative journey hasn't been a straight line, and that is my greatest asset. I'm currently studying Psychology at the University of Ibadan. This formal education gives me a framework for understanding audience empathy and behavior, which directly translates into my writing and editing.

Before this, I spent time exploring Biochemistry at Obafemi Awolowo University and spent a year working as a teacher, coaching primary school students for rigorous entrance exams. Teaching taught me the absolute necessity of clarity and simplicity in communication.

Professionally, I am building my foundation through real-world applications. From an intensive internship at Scaldis Creative Agency to ongoing volunteer work shaping the content strategy for the Greatness in Simplicity Initiative (GSI), I have learned to deliver assets that meet standards and hit deadlines.
